My work at BHP covered process diagnosis, an investment case for payroll systems, and delivery coordination for a mining asset-health initiative. These assignments involved working across business and technical teams to define the problem, agree a course of action, and move the work forward.

## An investment case grounded in the process

As a Specialist in Enterprise Improvement, I investigated systemic payroll issues across the Americas. I used root-cause analysis and process mapping to frame the problem and developed an Opportunity Assessment for the Global Project Prioritization Board.

The assessment secured **more than $5M in technology investment**. That figure describes approved funding. It is separate from any subsequent implementation savings.

The contribution was connecting the operational problem with an investment decision: explaining the causes of the issues clearly enough to support a proposed technology response and its prioritization. It is a part of improvement work that sits alongside building a model or automating a report.

## Coordinating an asset-health initiative

As an Agile Delivery Practitioner in BHP's Technology Digital Factory, I led delivery coordination for Asset Health–Escondida, an initiative supporting predictive models for mining-truck component failures. The work involved **more than 15 specialists**, with risks, blockers, and dependencies to manage through to operations.

My role focused on the team's delivery and its connection to the operating environment. The predictive models were a team effort. A model's usefulness depends on how people can incorporate its information into their work, as well as on its technical performance.

## Simplifying a training library

In an earlier HR Training Specialist assignment, I simplified a training library in SAP SuccessFactors, reducing its size by **58%**. This was a content and process improvement: reducing the material to be managed rather than adding another system.

The reduction describes the size of the library. It does not imply an equivalent reduction in training time or an increase in learning effectiveness.

## How this connects to my data-science work

These roles shaped how I approach technical projects. I start by understanding the process and the decision a team is trying to make. That helps define what information is needed, which constraints matter, and how a proposed change could be evaluated.

In my later work at Rio Tinto, that approach has carried into production forecasting, planning optimization, document search, and collections reporting. The tools vary; the work still needs a clear connection to the people using it.
